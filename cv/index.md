*2021–2024 · DOE project DE-EE0009804 · With General Electric*

*2022–2024 · DOE project DE-EE0010312*

Produced the mechanical design of a 400 kW solar thermal receiver to directly calcine calcium carbonate to calcium oxide. Led simulation and technology down-selection for conceptual designs, and built a laboratory to validate particle-flow and heat-transfer models.
