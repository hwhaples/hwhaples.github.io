## Keeping a heliostat field working

Heliostats are the mirrors that direct sunlight toward a solar receiver. Keeping them clean is part of keeping the plant working well. At Heliogen, I worked on a prototype vehicle that could take on that cleaning work autonomously.

The project was called **ICARUS**: Installation & Cleaning Autonomous Robot & Utility Solution. I worked on a small design team to build up the autonomous system from scratch and implement it in a real vehicle.

## My contribution: perception and guidance

To guide a vehicle through a solar field, you need to turn measurements of its surroundings into something it can act on. My work combined perception inputs, including LIDAR, RTK GPS, and ultrasonic sensing, to guide the prototype during cleaning operations. At the end of the project, ICARUS could successfully navigate and clean deployed heliostats with no driver input.
